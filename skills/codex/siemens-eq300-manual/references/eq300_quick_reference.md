## Core Safety and Bean Rules

- Use pure roasted espresso or fully automatic coffee-machine beans.
- Do not use syrup-coated, caramel-coated, sugar-treated beans, coffee powder, or very oily beans.
- Fill the tank daily with clean, non-carbonated tap water.
- Store beans sealed in a cool place. Beans can remain in the hopper for several days without losing aroma.

## Controls Mentioned in the Manual

The extracted text often loses icon glyphs. Rely on visible machine buttons when guiding the user:

- Power: turns appliance on/off.
- Espresso button: makes espresso.
- Long coffee / cafe crema button: makes long extraction coffee and acts as `<` back without saving in menus.
- Cappuccino button: makes cappuccino and navigates down in menus.
- Latte macchiato button: makes latte macchiato and acts as `ok` / save in menus.
- Milk froth button: froths milk.
- Bean/intensity button: cycles coffee strength.
- Service button: starts care programs.
- Double-cup button: makes two espresso or long coffees.

## First Setup

1. Press power.
2. Select display language with the navigation key until the desired language appears.
3. Confirm with `ok`.
4. Wait until the machine rinses and displays `Choose beverage`.
5. Check and set water hardness before normal use.

## Basic Settings

Change settings:

1. Hold the two setup keys shown on the machine/manual for at least 3 seconds.
2. Press the menu/navigation key repeatedly until the desired setting appears.
3. Press `ok`.
4. Change the value with the navigation key.
5. Press `ok` to save.
6. Display shows `Settings saved`.
7. Press `<` to exit.

Settings overview:

- `Language`: menu language.
- `Factory reset`: restores factory settings and deletes personal settings.
- `W. hardness`: local water hardness. Options: `W. hardness 1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `Water filter`, `H2O-Softener`.
- `Auto Stand-by`: delay after last drink before auto shutoff.
- `Sound`: `Sound on` / `Sound off`.

If no key is pressed for 90 seconds, the machine returns to drink selection without saving.

## Water Hardness and Filter

Manual statements:

- Check and set water hardness before using the machine.
- Ask the local water utility for hardness.
- If using a water filter, choose `Water filter` and run the filter rinse/activation procedure.
- If the home has a water softening system, choose `H2O-Softener`.
- If Calc'nClean is requested very frequently, install a new water filter and set hardness appropriately.

Practical hardness mapping for BSH/Siemens automatic coffee machines when the manual does not show a table:

- `W. hardness 1`: about 1-7 °dH, roughly 10-130 mg/L as CaCO3.
- `W. hardness 2`: about 8-14 °dH, roughly 140-250 mg/L as CaCO3.
- `W. hardness 3`: about 15-21 °dH, roughly 260-380 mg/L as CaCO3.
- `W. hardness 4`: about 22-30 °dH, roughly 390-540 mg/L as CaCO3.

Treat this mapping as cross-model BSH guidance, not directly printed in this Chinese PDF. If a user has a water test strip, prefer the test strip result. If local water is not known, recommend asking the utility for total hardness in mg/L as CaCO3.

### Water Filter Activation/Replacement

1. Turn on the machine.
2. Hold the two setup keys at least 3 seconds.
3. Navigate until `W. hardness` appears and confirm with `ok`.
4. Navigate until `Water filter` appears and confirm with `ok`.
5. Display shows `Filter act.`.
6. Immerse the filter in a glass of water with opening upward; gently press sides until bubbles stop.
7. Set water hardness on the filter aroma ring.
8. Press the filter firmly into the empty water tank.
9. Fill water tank to `max` and insert it.
10. Place a container of at least 1 L under the outlet.
11. Press `ok` to save and start rinsing.
12. Press `<` to exit; empty the container.

Replace the water filter for hygiene and at least every 2 months, or when the display indicates replacement.

## Drink Setup and Strength

Coffee strength levels, in order:

- Very mild
- Mild
- Normal
- Strong
- Very strong
- Aroma intense, model-dependent

The selected strength applies to all coffee-containing drinks.

### Cup Volume

1. Place a cup under the outlet.
2. Hold the drink key, e.g. espresso or long coffee, for 3 seconds.
3. Drink is prepared and selected key flashes; display shows progress.
4. Press the same drink key again when the desired volume is reached.
5. New volume applies from the next drink.

Limits:

- Espresso: 25-60 ml.
- Long coffee/cafe crema: 80-200 ml.
- Machine always extracts at least 25 ml espresso or 80 ml long coffee.

### Milk Froth Time

1. Add about 100 ml milk for cappuccino or about 150 ml for latte macchiato.
2. Place cup/glass under outlet.
3. Push outlet and milk frother all the way down.
4. Hold the relevant milk drink/froth key for 3 seconds.
5. Press the same key again when desired frothing time is reached.
6. Froth-time setting does not affect coffee cup volume.

## Grinder Adjustment

Only adjust while the grinder is running. Turn only step by step. New setting takes effect after the second coffee.

- Finer grind: turn counterclockwise. Manual says finer grind is suitable for lighter-roasted beans.
- Coarser grind: turn clockwise. Manual says coarser grind is suitable for darker-roasted beans.

Taste troubleshooting:

- Coffee drips, does not flow, volume not reached, too bitter, burnt taste: grind is too fine; adjust coarser.
- Coffee flows too fast, crema too low, sour, thin, watery: adjust finer.
- No crema: coffee type may be unsuitable, beans stale, or grind too coarse; use fresher/deeper beans or adjust finer.

For dark espresso beans used for Americano/long black: start at factory middle or slightly coarse, strength `Strong`, espresso 35-40 ml, dilute with hot water to taste. Avoid pulling very long cups through the puck because it can become thin and bitter.

## Two-Cup Function

1. Press two-cup key; display shows `Double cup on` for 5 seconds.
2. Put one cup under each side of the outlet.
3. Press espresso or long coffee.
4. Drink is prepared in two grinding/extraction steps.
5. Press two-cup key again to disable.

Only espresso or long coffee supports two-cup. If no key is pressed for 90 seconds, the machine automatically switches to `Double cup off`.

## Cleaning and Care

Dishwasher-safe components, max 60 °C program:

- Drip plate.
- Splash guard.
- Coffee grounds container.
- Water level indicator.
- Milk frother.

Not dishwasher-safe:

- Water tank.
- Water tank lid.
- Bean container aroma lid.
- Brew unit.
- Drip tray.

Cleaning rules:

- Do not use abrasive/corrosive cleaners, alcohol/benzene cleaners, hard scouring pads, steam cleaners, or pressure washers.
- Do not descale with pure citric acid, vinegar, vinegar-based products, or phosphoric-acid descalers.
- Use Siemens/BSH cleaning tablets and descaling tablets developed for the machine.
- Wipe outlet after drink preparation.
- Clean and empty drip tray and grounds container daily.
- Clean the brew unit regularly by removing it; do not use detergent, vinegar/acid cleaners, or dishwasher.
- Clean the milk frother after every use.

Accessories listed in the manual:

- Cleaning tablets: TZ80001A / TZ80001B; customer service 00312097 / 00312098.
- Descaling tablets: TZ80002A / TZ80002B; customer service 00312094 / 00312095.
- Water filter: TZ70003C; customer service 17007590.
- 3-pack water filter: TZ70033A.
- Microfiber cloth: 00460770.
- Care set: TZ80004A / TZ80004B; customer service 00312105 / 00312106.

## Calc'nClean

When the service key lights and display says `Calc'nClean required!`, run the care program promptly. If not done, the machine can lock with `Device locked`; run care program to unlock.

Before starting: remove, clean, and reinsert the brew unit; clean the milk frother.

Program duration: about 40 minutes.

Procedure summary:

1. Hold service key at least 5 seconds.
2. Open the door.
3. Remove and empty drip tray and grounds container.
4. Remove and clean brew unit.
5. Put one cleaning tablet into brew unit tablet slot and reinsert brew unit.
6. Reinsert drip tray and grounds container; close door.
7. Remove water filter if installed.
8. Fill empty water tank with lukewarm water to 0.5 L mark and dissolve one Siemens descaling tablet.
9. Insert tank; place about 1 L container under outlet.
10. If prompt flashes, press the indicated key to start cleaning/descaling. First phase about 20 minutes.
11. Empty container; open door.
12. Empty and reinsert drip tray.
13. Close door; put container back under outlet.
14. Clean water tank; reinstall water filter if removed.
15. Fill tank with clean non-carbonated tap water to `max`.
16. Press indicated flashing key to start rinse.
17. Remove and empty drip tray and grounds container.
18. Clean and reinsert them. Machine is ready.

If care program is interrupted by power failure etc. and service/indicator symbols light:

1. Clean water tank and reinstall filter if removed.
2. Fill tank with clean non-carbonated tap water to `max`.
3. Press indicated key; rinse takes about 2 minutes.
4. Empty, clean, and reinsert drip tray.
5. Close door.

## Troubleshooting Quick Reference

- Machine does not respond: unplug, wait 60 seconds, plug back in.
- Only water, no coffee: add beans; beans may be too oily and not falling into grinder; tap hopper lightly; change bean type; clean empty hopper with damp cloth; check brew unit inserted fully.
- Coffee/milk foam quality unstable: descale.
- No coffee or only drips / volume not reached: grind too fine, adjust coarser; descale; remove air from filter; clean brew unit; run Calc'nClean; clean tank if descaler residue blocks it; lower strength if set too high.
- Milk drink too hot or foam not creamy and suction loud: too little milk; use more milk or change frothing time.
- Milk drink too cold: too much milk or milk too cold; use less or warmer milk; change frothing time.
- No crema: bean type unsuitable; use more Robusta or deeper roast; use fresh beans; adjust finer.
- Coffee too sour: grind too coarse; adjust finer; use more Robusta or darker roast.
- Coffee too bitter: grind too fine; adjust coarser; change bean type.
- Coffee tastes burnt: grind too fine; adjust coarser; change bean type.
- No milk foam: descale; clean milk frother; use more milk; ensure frother is immersed; push outlet down fully.
- No drink: air in water filter or filter not pressed vertically into tank connector; clean tank if descaler residue blocks it.
- Water inside bottom: drip tray removed too early; wait a few seconds after final drink before removing tray.
- Brew unit cannot be removed: switch off, wait 3 minutes, switch on again.
- Grinder does not start: machine overheated; unplug and let cool 1 hour.
- `Fill water tank` although full: insert tank correctly; use non-carbonated water; clean stuck float; rinse/install new filter properly; replace old filter; clean tank and run Calc'nClean if limescale blocks system.
- `Clean brewing unit`: clean brew unit.
- `Incorrect voltage`: operate only at 220-240 V.
- Frequent `Calc'nClean required!`: water still contains lime; install new filter and set hardness; use proper descaling tablets.
- Cannot operate / display changes: demo mode; hold indicated key at least 5 seconds, remove tank, fill with clean water, reinsert.
- `Close door` although closed: press door closed; if overheated, unplug and cool 1 hour.
- `Empty trays` although emptied: machine was off and did not detect emptying; switch on, remove drip tray, reinsert.
- `Fill water tank` and LED flashes: tank missing/incorrect, pipes dry, or filter contains air.
- `Device too cold`: operate above 5 °C.

## Transport / Storage Antifreeze Protection

Use before transport/storage in freezing conditions.

Requirements: machine ready for use and water tank filled.

1. Place large container under outlet and push milk frother down.
2. Hold indicated steam/froth key at least 5 seconds.
3. When steam escapes from frother, remove water tank.
4. Let steam fully escape until display shows `Water tank empty`.
5. Turn off with power.
6. Empty tank and drip tray; clean machine thoroughly.
7. Unplug. Brew unit is locked and cannot be removed during this state.
